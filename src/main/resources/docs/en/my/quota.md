# My Quota

> What I used this month and what is left, at a glance.

## What this page does

This is the page you land on when you open the console. It shows **your own** numbers only. Quota and keys are at the top, monthly usage and available models in the middle, then a daily spend chart and your recent calls.

## When you need it

- Check how much quota is left this month
- See which models you may use
- Confirm your key and models are set up when a tool fails
- See whether recent calls succeeded

## What is on this page

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly quota bar | Used versus remaining; resets on the 1st of each month |
| My access keys | Your keys; copying here gives a masked value that cannot be used to call the service |
| Models I can use | The virtual model names you may put into your AI tool |
| Daily spend chart | How much you spent each day this month |
| Recent calls | Time, model, cost, and success or failure |

## Common tasks

**Check your remaining quota**

1. Open this page and read the monthly quota bar
2. For details, click through to My Bills

**First time here and everything is empty**

1. Follow the link to My Keys
2. Send the prepared request text to your administrator
3. Refresh once the key is issued

**Try whether it works**

1. Open Playground
2. Pick a model and send a message

## If something goes wrong

**The bar is empty or amounts are zero**

Either you have not made a call this month, or prices have not been configured yet. A zero amount does not stop you from using the service.

**Nothing happens when I request more quota**

Online approval is not connected yet. Ask your administrator to adjust your quota directly.

**The copied key does not work**

This page copies a masked value for identification only. Get the full key from My Keys using the copy icon on that row.

## Still stuck?

Tell your administrator your account name, the model name you want, and the exact message shown on the page.
